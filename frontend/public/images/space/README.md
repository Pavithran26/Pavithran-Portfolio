# Original worlds

These three fictional worlds were created for Pavithran's portfolio with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool. They are original atmospheric artwork, not NASA photography or geographically accurate representations of Earth or other planets. No artwork was copied from the design reference.

| File | Dimensions | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ocean-world.webp` | 1254 × 1254 | Opening horizon and frontend world |
| `rocky-world.webp` | 1254 × 1254 | Backend and logic world |
| `ring-world.webp` | 1536 × 1024 | Databases and persistence world |

The three source PNGs were converted to WebP at quality 88 with their transparent alpha preserved. Combined transfer size is approximately 892 KiB. The complete planet and ring silhouettes are retained; the opening scene intentionally enlarges the ocean world beyond the viewport. The ring world should always keep its natural aspect ratio.

Exact generation prompts are recorded in `generation-prompts.json`. Technology logos remain in `../../icons/technologies/` with their existing source attribution and licenses.
